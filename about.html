Meet Dr. Anjali Deshmukh

Dr. Anjali Deshmukh is a dedicated General Physician practicing in Pune, Maharashtra. She believes that good medicine begins with attentive listening. In her consultations, patients are given the time and space to express their symptoms and concerns without feeling rushed, ensuring an accurate clinical evaluation and a warm, supportive atmosphere.

Her clinical practice focuses on thorough diagnostic assessments, evidence-based treatment plans, and continuous guidance for everyday health, chronic condition management, and preventive wellness. By partnering closely with patients, she helps individuals make informed decisions about their health with clarity and confidence.
